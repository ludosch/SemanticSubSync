Re-times the external .srt subtitles of movies and episodes on the subtitle embedded in the video, by matching lines on meaning. When too few lines match, the file is left alone.

Any movie or episode: Sync subtitles in its menu. Chosen libraries: automatically, when a video is added and with the scheduled task Re-time new subtitles (Dashboard > Scheduled Tasks).

The engine and its model (about 400 MB) are installed in the plugin's data folder, in the background, a few minutes after Jellyfin starts. Jellyfin needs write access to the media folders.
